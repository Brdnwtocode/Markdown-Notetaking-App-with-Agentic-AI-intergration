# C2-02 — How People Use AI Today

## The One Big Idea
AI adoption follows a bell curve: a small minority under-uses it for basic prompts, the vast majority stays in passive chat mode, and only a rare few reach agentic workflows where AI executes real operations. Lock In is designed to move users from the middle to the right.

## Visual Concept
A bell curve chart spanning the slide width. Three labeled zones color the curve: left tail (gray — "Under-use"), middle bulge (neutral — "Passive Chat"), right tail (emerald — "Agentic Workflows"). Below each zone, a one-line example of what that usage looks like. An arrow points from the middle toward the right tail — "Lock In moves users here."

## Content Elements

### Headline
The AI Adoption Bell Curve

### Three Zones

**Left Tail — Under-Use**

"Write me an email." Basic prompting, no tool integration, no context.

**The Middle — Passive Chat (Where Most Users Are Stuck)**

"Summarize this document." "Answer this question." ChatGPT-style QA and drafting — but the AI never touches your actual data or applications.

**Right Tail — Agentic Workflows (Where Lock In Takes You)**

"Add the meeting notes to my project Stack, extract action items as tasks, and schedule the follow-up for Friday." AI understands your workspace, proposes structured changes, and you approve.
