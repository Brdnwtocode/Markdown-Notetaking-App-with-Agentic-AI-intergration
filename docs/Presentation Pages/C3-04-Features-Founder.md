# C3-04 — Features for the Founder & Business Owner

## The One Big Idea
A founder stops losing ideas to scattered voice memos and messaging apps — and gains a centralized business operating system where thoughts are captured instantly, structured by AI, and queried conversationally across all company knowledge.

## Visual Concept
A "Before → After" horizontal transformation. Left side: chaotic — phone with voice memos, chat apps, sticky notes — ideas disappearing. Right side: structured workflow — Voice Input (anywhere) → Records (transcription archive) → Agentic Automate (AI structuring) → Stacks (pipeline/experiments) → AI Companion (conversational business queries). A rocket icon at the end symbolizes traction and momentum.

## Content Elements

### Headline
From Fleeting Thoughts to a Central Business Operating System

### The Pain (Before)
Ideas emerge during meetings, customer calls, or while traveling. Captured in scattered voice memos and messaging apps. No structure. No follow-through. Insights lost in the daily operational noise.

### The Lock In Workflow (After)

| Step | Module | What Happens |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Voice Input** | Capture thoughts immediately via push-to-talk — works anywhere, even between appointments |
| 2 | **Records** | Automatic transcription creates a searchable archive of every business discussion and idea |
| 3 | **Agentic Automate** | AI organizes raw thoughts into structured sections: Opportunities, Customer Problems, Proposed Solutions, Risks, Next Steps |
| 4 | **Stacks** | Build your operating system: Customer Pipeline (Company, Contact, Value, Stage), Growth Experiments (Hypothesis, KPI, Outcome), Partnership Tracking (Partner, Status, Priority) |
| 5 | **AI Companion** | Ask conversationally: "Which opportunities need attention this week?" "What customer pain points appear most frequently?" "Summarize key decisions made this month." |

### Key Expectations Delivered
Instant voice capture anywhere ✓ AI structuring of raw thoughts ✓ Pipeline and experiment tracking ✓ Conversational business intelligence ✓
