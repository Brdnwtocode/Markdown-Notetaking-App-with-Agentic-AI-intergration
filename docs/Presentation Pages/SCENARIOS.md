# **Scenario 1: HR & Talent Development Lead**

As an HR and Talent Development Lead, I spend a significant amount of time reviewing applications, conducting interviews, coordinating with hiring managers, and ensuring that recruitment decisions are made fairly and consistently. 

\- One of my biggest challenges is that candidate information often exists in different formats and locations. CVs are stored as PDFs, interview feedback is scattered across notes, and hiring progress is tracked separately in spreadsheets or project management tools. Moving information between these systems creates unnecessary administrative work and increases the risk of inconsistencies. To streamline the process, I use **Stack Creation** to build a structured candidate evaluation database with fields such as:

* Candidate Name  
* Position Applied  
* Years of Experience  
* Skills  
* Interview Score  
* Hiring Recommendation

*Rather than manually entering information, I would like to drag and drop multiple resumes directly into the Stack and have AI automatically extract and populate relevant information into the appropriate fields.*

\- After interviews are completed, I upload recordings into **Records**, where they are automatically transcribed into searchable transcripts. Using **Agentic Automate**, I generate candidate summaries, extract strengths and weaknesses, and identify important discussion points from each interview. *A valuable enhancement would be an **Extract to Stack** workflow that automatically transfers interview insights into the evaluation database and generates preliminary scoring recommendations.*

To manage recruitment execution, I rely on **Sprint Task Execution** to track activities such as:

* CV screening  
* Interview scheduling  
* Offer preparation  
* Onboarding readiness

\- While I want recruitment progress to be transparent across the hiring team, certain information must remain confidential. *Fine-grained permission controls for Notes, Records, and attached documents would allow me to balance collaboration with privacy.*

**Key expectations**

* Bulk CV import into Stack  
* AI-powered candidate profiling and scoring  
* Extract-to-Stack automation  
* Candidate comparison dashboards  
* Role-based access and permissions

# **Scenario 2: Academic Tutor**

As an academic tutor, I constantly switch between lesson planning, student assessment, curriculum development, and schedule management.

A typical week involves preparing study materials, reviewing assignments, evaluating speaking performance, and coordinating multiple classes across different subjects. One of my biggest challenges is keeping teaching resources organized. Lesson plans, worksheets, reference materials, and student feedback are often scattered across different folders and platforms, making it difficult to quickly retrieve information when I need it.

\- I use **Notes** as the central knowledge hub for my teaching activities. Lesson plans, teaching resources, classroom activities, assessment rubrics, and revision materials can all be stored within the same workspace and organized in a structured manner. Instead of searching through multiple applications or folders, I can use the **AI Companion** to instantly retrieve information from my notes, summarize previous materials, or generate supporting content based on existing lesson resources.

\- For speaking assessments, students submit their recordings and I upload them into **Records**, where the system automatically generates transcripts alongside the original audio. Instead of manually reviewing every recording from start to finish, I use the **AI Companion** to analyze the transcript and identify:

* Grammar mistakes  
* Vocabulary misuse  
* Awkward sentence structures  
* Areas for improvement

The AI acts as an instructional assistant, helping me provide more detailed, consistent, and objective feedback while significantly reducing review time.

\- When lesson plans are finalized, I use **Agentic Automate** to extract actionable items directly from my notes. Homework assignments, revision activities, mock tests, and administrative follow-ups can be converted into tasks without additional manual work.

\- To manage my workload, I organize all teaching activities within the **Calendar**, using weekly planning to visualize:

* Teaching sessions  
* Student consultations  
* Assessment deadlines  
* Administrative responsibilities

*Color-coded schedules would make it easier to distinguish between different subjects and programs at a glance.*

**Key expectations**

* AI pronunciation analysis  
* Inline transcript corrections  
* Student progress dashboards  
* Recurring lesson templates  
* Color-coded calendars  
* AI-generated learning recommendations

# **Scenario 3: Founder & Small Business Owner**

As a founder, I am involved in almost every aspect of the business, from strategy and product development to sales, partnerships, and operations.

My biggest challenge is rarely generating ideas. The real challenge is ensuring that important insights, decisions, and opportunities are captured, organized, and converted into action before they get lost in daily operations. 

\- Throughout the day, ideas often emerge during meetings, customer conversations, or while travelling between appointments. Instead of relying on scattered voice notes and messaging apps, I use **Records** and the **AI Companion's Voice Input** to capture thoughts immediately. These recordings are automatically converted into transcripts, creating a searchable archive of business discussions and ideas.

\- When reviewing recordings, I use **Agentic Automate** to transform raw thoughts into structured documentation. AI organizes information into sections such as:

* Business opportunities  
* Customer problems  
* Proposed solutions  
* Risks and assumptions  
* Recommended next steps

This allows me to build a growing knowledge base rather than accumulating disconnected notes.

\- I also use **Stack Creation** to manage key business information, including:

**Customer Pipeline**

* Company  
* Contact  
* Opportunity Value  
* Current Stage  
* Next Action

**Growth Experiments**

* Hypothesis  
* KPI  
* Outcome  
* Decision

**Partnership Tracking**

* Partner  
* Status  
* Priority  
* Follow-up Date

This transforms Lock In from a note-taking application into a centralized operating system for running the business.

\- To maintain visibility across everything happening in the company, I combine **Tasks**, **Calendar**, **Notes**, **Records**, and **Stacks** within a single workspace. Instead of searching through multiple applications, I can ask the **AI Companion** questions such as:

* Which opportunities require attention this week?  
* What customer pain points appear most frequently across recent discussions?  
* Which growth experiments have produced the strongest results?  
* Summarize key decisions made this month.

The ability to interact conversationally with all business knowledge significantly reduces the time spent searching for information and increases the time spent making decisions.

**Key expectations**

* AI-generated executive dashboards  
* Automated KPI tracking from Stack data  
* Daily founder briefings  
* Meeting-to-task automation  
* AI-powered opportunity prioritization  
* Business intelligence across Notes, Records, Tasks, and Stacks  
* Executive decision logs generated automatically
