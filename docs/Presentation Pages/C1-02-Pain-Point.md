# C1-02 — The Pain Point

## The One Big Idea
Three measurable problems define the modern knowledge worker's struggle: tools fragment attention (97% productivity loss), typing bottlenecks thought (3× slower than speech), and AI remains locked in passive chat instead of actively helping.

## Visual Concept
Three large icon-anchored cards in a horizontal row, each with a giant statistic number. The numbers animate from zero to their target values when the slide enters view. Each card has a single-line explanation below the number. The cards are dark surface panels with thin neutral borders.

## Content Elements

### Headline
The Friction of Thought

### Card 1: Fragmentation
- **Big Number**: 97%
- **Icon**: Puzzle pieces (split/fragmented)
- **Supporting line**: of people lose productivity when context-switching between fragmented digital tools

### Card 2: The Typing Bottleneck
- **Big Number**: 3×
- **Icon**: Turtle vs rabbit (speed contrast)
- **Supporting line**: throughput gap — manual typing at 40–60 WPM versus conversational speech at 125–150 WPM

### Card 3: Passive AI
- **Icon**: Chat bubble locked in a box
- **Label**: Stuck in Chat
- **Supporting line**: Current AI assistants are passive chatbots — they generate text but cannot execute workspace operations

## Interactive Notes
- Numbers count up from 0: 97% animates upward in increments, 3× counts from 1.0 to 3.0
- Cards fade in sequentially (staggered entrance)
