# C2-04 — The Information → Context → Knowledge Friction

## The One Big Idea
Raw information is abundant and overwhelming. Actionable knowledge is scarce and valuable. The bottleneck is the middle step — context — the cognitive work of organizing, linking, filtering, and understanding relationships. Most tools leave users stranded here, doing the connecting manually.

## Visual Concept
A large funnel diagram dominating the slide. Wide at the top, progressively narrowing to a focused point at the bottom. Three labeled layers inside the funnel. The middle layer (Context) has a visual "friction" indicator — sparking, grinding — showing that this is where users get stuck. The funnel's narrow bottom glows emerald: Knowledge.

## Content Elements

### Headline
The Bottleneck Is Not Information. It's Context.

### The Funnel (three layers)

**Top — INFORMATION (Wide, Overwhelming)**

Raw data, scattered notes, meeting transcripts, emails, voice memos, PDFs, spreadsheets. Abundant. Unstructured. Easy to collect, hard to use.

**Middle — CONTEXT (The Bottleneck — Where Users Struggle)**

Organizing. Linking. Filtering. Understanding relationships between pieces of information. This requires cognitive effort — and most tools provide zero assistance. Users do this work manually, every time.

**Bottom — KNOWLEDGE (Narrow, Valuable)**

Actionable insight. Decisions. Structured records. Completed tasks. This is the goal — but reaching it requires crossing the Context gap.

### The Cost (one line below the funnel)
Time lost. Mental fatigue. Insights forgotten. Duplicated effort. Opportunities missed.
