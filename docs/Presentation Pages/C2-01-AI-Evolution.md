# C2-01 — The Evolution of AI

## The One Big Idea
AI has progressed through five distinct evolutionary stages — from simple pattern classifiers to collaborative multi-agent systems — and Lock In operates at the frontier: stages 3 and 4, where AI reasons, uses tools, and orchestrates multiple specialized experts.

## Visual Concept
A horizontal timeline illustration with five stepping-stone nodes, connected by arrows. Each node has an icon, a stage name, a year range, and a one-line capability description. The timeline ascends visually from left to right. Stages 3 and 4 are highlighted in emerald — that's where Lock In lives.

## Content Elements

### Headline
From Pattern Matching to Agentic Reasoning

### Timeline (5 stages)

| Stage | Icon | Era | Capability |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Classifiers** | Magnifying glass | 2010s | Pattern matching — "This is a cat" |
| **2. LLMs** | Document/text | 2020+ | Language understanding — "Write an essay about cats" |
| **3. Agents** ✦ | Robot/gear | 2023+ | Tool-using AI — "Search the web for cat breeds and summarize" |
| **4. Multi-Agent** ✦ | Network/nodes | 2024+ | Specialized agents collaborating — one researches, one critiques, one writes |
| **5. AGI** | Brain/star | Future | General intelligence across all domains |

✦ = Lock In operates here, using LangGraph to orchestrate multiple expert agents in parallel
