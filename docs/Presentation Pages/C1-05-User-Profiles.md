# C1-05 — Three User Profiles

## The One Big Idea
Lock In is not built for a hypothetical "everyone." It is designed for three real user profiles — each with distinct pain points, workflows, and expectations — validated through detailed scenario analysis.

## Visual Concept
Three persona hero cards in a row. Each card features: an icon representing the role, the role title, a pain statement (in the user's voice), and a "desires" line. Clean, human-centered, minimal. The cards are equal weight — no persona is the "primary" user.

## Content Elements

### Headline
Three Users. One Workspace.

### Persona 1: HR & Talent Development Lead
- **Icon**: Briefcase / people
- **Pain**: "Candidate data is scattered across PDFs, interview notes, and spreadsheets. I spend more time moving information than making decisions."
- **Desires**: Bulk CV import into structured tables. AI-powered candidate profiling. Interview insights extracted automatically.

### Persona 2: Academic Tutor
- **Icon**: Graduation cap / book
- **Pain**: "Lesson plans, student assessments, and schedules live in different folders and apps. Reviewing speaking recordings manually takes hours."
- **Desires**: AI analysis of student speaking assessments. Central lesson hub. Automatic task extraction from lesson plans.

### Persona 3: Founder & Business Owner
- **Icon**: Lightbulb / rocket
- **Pain**: "Ideas come anywhere, anytime — during meetings, while traveling. If I don't capture and structure them immediately, they're gone."
- **Desires**: Instant voice capture. AI structuring of raw thoughts into business records. Conversational querying across all company knowledge.

## Interactive Notes

- Each card shows the persona icon large, then the quote
- Human-centered, emotionally resonant — not a spec sheet
