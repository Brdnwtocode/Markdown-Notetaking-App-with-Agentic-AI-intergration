# C1-04 — Existing Solutions & Remaining Gaps

## The One Big Idea
Notion and Obsidian are excellent tools — but each leaves a critical gap. Notion's AI can act autonomously (no mandatory safety gate). Obsidian's AI depends on third-party plugins (no native intelligence). Lock In fills the gap: deeply integrated, context-aware AI that proposes — but you decide.

## Visual Concept
Three clean comparison cards side by side — Notion, Obsidian, Lock In. Each card has the product icon/name at top, three strength bullet points, and one highlighted "gap" statement at the bottom. Lock In's card has a subtle emerald glow. No dense table — spacious, scannable, visual.

## Content Elements

### Headline
Great Tools. One Missing Piece.

### Notion Card
- **Identity**: All-in-one team workspace
- **Strengths**: Powerful databases with 6 views, deep AI agents that act autonomously, real-time collaboration
- **The Gap**: AI can act without you — no mandatory human confirmation gate. Databases take weeks to master.

### Obsidian Card
- **Identity**: Local-first personal knowledge vault
- **Strengths**: Plain Markdown files you own forever, 4,000+ community plugins, full offline support
- **The Gap**: AI depends entirely on third-party plugins — quality and safety vary. No native structured data or voice pipeline.

### Lock In Card (highlighted)
- **Identity**: AI-native individual workspace
- **Strengths**: Schema-aware AI that respects your data, voice-driven at 3× typing speed, human-in-the-loop on every change
- **What It Fills**: The only tool where AI deeply understands your notes AND tables, proposes changes via voice, and never writes without your approval.
